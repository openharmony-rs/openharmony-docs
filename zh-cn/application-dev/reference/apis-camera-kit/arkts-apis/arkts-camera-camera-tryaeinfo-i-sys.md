# TryAEInfo（系统接口）

Describes the Try AE parameters. Try AE indicates that the hardware reports the status based on the ambient illumination change during time-lapse photographing.

**起始版本：** 12

**ArkTS模式：** ArkTS-Dyn起始版本为12；ArkTS-Sta起始版本为23。
