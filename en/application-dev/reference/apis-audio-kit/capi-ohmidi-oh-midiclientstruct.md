## Overview

Declares a MIDI client. OH_MIDIClient is used to establish a connection with the MIDI service and serves as the basic handle for developers to perform operations such as MIDI data transmission and reception and device management. It applies to scenarios that require communication with MIDI devices, such as music players, MIDI controller access, and digital music creation.
