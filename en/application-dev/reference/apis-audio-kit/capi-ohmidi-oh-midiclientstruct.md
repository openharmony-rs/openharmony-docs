## Overview

Declares a MIDI client. OH_MIDIClient is used to establish a connection with the MIDI service and serves as the base handle for developers to perform operations such as MIDI data sending and receiving and device management. It applies to scenarios that require communication with MIDI devices, such as music players, MIDI controller access, and digital music creation.
