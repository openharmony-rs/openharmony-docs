## Overview

RawFile represents an opened rawfile object. It is obtained by calling [OH_ResourceManager_OpenRawFile](capi-raw-file-manager-h.md#oh_resourcemanager_openrawfile) and must be closed and released by calling [OH_ResourceManager_CloseRawFile](capi-raw-file-h.md#oh_resourcemanager_closerawfile) after use.
