## Overview

Describes the filter used to filter audio and video content. You can configure filtering rules to filter and control audio and video streams in screen capture content to meet content processing requirements in different scenarios.

This API is applicable to scenarios such as privacy protection (for example, filtering sensitive pages) and excluding audio and video of specified apps, effectively improving the controllability of screen recording content.
