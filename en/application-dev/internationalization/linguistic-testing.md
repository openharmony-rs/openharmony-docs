Linguistic testing takes place after application internationalization and localization and before the official release. Local user experts inspect whether the UI and content comply with local usage habits.

In a multilingual environment, localization quality is critical to product acceptance. The professionalism of UI content, consistency of translations, wording style, and UI presentation all affect the user experience; even minor errors may cause users to stop using the app. Before a global release, use linguistic testing to identify and fix potential issues and improve the experience of users worldwide. Also review sensitive and taboo content: geopolitically sensitive terms, prohibited terms, and terms that require caution can have a significant business impact. A comprehensive approach to handling sensitive terminology helps safeguard international product launches.
